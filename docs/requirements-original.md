AI SIGN LANGUAGE TRANSLATOR

1. About the Project

- An educational project that demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence can recognise American Sign Language (ASL) hand signs.
- Users can show their hand signs to a camera, and the AI analyses the image to identify the sign.
- The website displays the recognised sign, its meaning and the confidence percentage.
- The project supports nine selected static hand signs.
- The website has a simple, attractive and user-friendly design.
- The entire project is displayed on one single webpage.
- The website uses a pink, yellow and white colour theme.
- The data should be saved locally so that the information remains available even after closing or reopening the website . 

2. How It Works – User's Point of View

1. The user opens the website.
2. At the top, they see the project title AI SIGN LANGUAGE TRANSLATOR.
3. Below the title, they see "WELCOME!" and their participation number.
4. On the left side, they see a camera window with a CLICK TO START button.
5. After clicking the button, the camera turns on.
6. The user shows one of the supported ASL hand signs to the camera.
7. The user captures their hand sign.
8. The AI analyses the captured image, and an analysing animation appears on the screen.
9. On the right side of the camera window, the website displays statistics showing:
   - Total users
   - Total attempts
   - Correct recognitions
   - Incorrect recognitions
10. Below the camera window, the website displays the analysis result.

If the sign is successfully recognised:

- Display the message "THE AI HAS SUCCESSFULLY RECOGNISED THE SIGN!"
- Show the recognised letter or expression.
- Show its meaning.
- Display the confidence percentage.

If the sign is not recognised:

- Display the message "THE AI FAILED TO RECOGNISE THE SIGN."
- If the sign is not included in the project, display "SIGN NOT INCLUDED IN THIS PROJECT."
- Ask the user to try again.

11. The user can confirm whether the prediction is correct.
12. The statistics are updated accordingly.
13. The user can choose to try another scan or finish.

3. Website Appearance

- The project title is displayed at the top of the webpage.
- Below it is the welcome message and participation number.
- The main section has a camera window on the left and a statistics section on the right.
- The AI analysing animation appears during image recognition.
